# Stress check-ins

Open **Record → Check-ins → Stress Check-in**. Choose your overall stress from
**None (0)**, **Mild (1)**, **Moderate (2)**, **High (3)**, or **Overwhelming (4)**.
The question is how much stress you feel right now. Overall stress works alone;
there is no requirement to name or rate individual stressors.

In **Manage Stressors**, add any number of personal situations you find useful
to track. Starting with a few, such as one to three, can make regular check-ins
easier. Reorder them using Edit. Rename or archive them as your circumstances
change; archiving leaves earlier answers available in history, and earlier
check-ins retain the original names and identities.

Each new form begins without answers. For a personal stressor, **None** records
an explicit zero. **Not applicable** and **Skipped** have no numeric rating.
Leaving an item unanswered saves it as Skipped. The app never fills missing
answers with zero or copies answers from your previous check-in. The overall
rating is required to save. A check-in and all its individual answers save
together.

Automatic timestamps and Now retain full precision. Ordinary manual time choices
clear seconds and fractions. Editing a historical check-in keeps its original
time unless you choose to change it, and retains the stressors originally
offered rather than adding your current list retroactively.

The zeroing rules preserve a signal for possible estimated times. See
[Timestamp precision, accuracy estimates and forgetfulness proxies](timestamp-precision.md)
for how to use that signal, its limits, and the distinction between an event's
time and when it was entered.

## Evaluate a change

For example, record before and after a workplace adjustment, a change in support,
or a new routine. **Stress Review** can compare seven calendar days before a
chosen date with seven days starting on that date. Review overall stress and
each relevant personal stressor separately, alongside the dates and response
coverage. An incomplete period is identified. Other changes may also affect
the pattern; a comparison does not establish what caused it.

Recent reviews offer 7, 30 and 90 calendar days, including an incomplete today.
Dates follow stored instants in the current device calendar and time zone,
including daylight-saving changes. Starts are inclusive and ends exclusive;
future-dated check-ins are excluded. Missing days remain missing.

The five anchor counts show the distribution of valid answers. Daily averages
give each day with valid ratings equal weight; per-check-in averages are shown
separately. These describe positions on a subjective ordinal scale, not a
validated clinical score. No total is formed by adding personal stressors.
Skipped, not-applicable and invalid answers are disclosed and excluded from
rating averages. A stressor added later has only the responses actually offered
in that period; earlier check-ins are not invented for it.

## Prepare for an appointment

Choose a useful review period, then open **Appointment Prep**. Review dates,
recurring pressures, coverage and notes, and choose the examples or questions
you want to discuss. Sharing a summary is an explicit action. It may include
personal stressor names and notes, so review the content before sharing.

The brief first-use introduction can be dismissed. **Stress Guide** remains
available from Check-ins and the form's help button to explain these use cases
again. Check-ins and calculations remain on-device.

## Optional reminders, queries and export

**Stress Reminders** provides an optional daily prompt with quiet hours and an
option to skip the rest of a day after a valid stress check-in. Mood recording
does not complete a stress check-in or suppress its prompts. Notification text
contains no ratings, notes or personal stressor names. The app schedules the
next 14 days; reopen it at least every 14 days and after travelling to refresh
the schedule. Existing mood reminders retain their separate settings.

Overall check-ins and individual stressor responses are available in Explore's
query builder and Settings' CSV exports. Individual exports retain check-in and
stressor identifiers, historical names, response states and blank numeric values
for Skipped and Not applicable. **Timestamp Reference Seconds** preserves the
stored timestamp's full precision for accuracy analysis; the linked precision
guide explains its epoch and reconstruction. Existing saved data type identifiers
are preserved. Editing an individual query result opens its whole check-in;
deleting a parent check-in deletes its individual answers together.
